Why it matters

When the monitors went dark

For many cities in Africa and Asia, the US Department of State’s embassy monitors were the only public, reference-grade PM2.5 measurements. Most of them have stopped reporting.

AirTrace

Daily PM2.5 forecasts that correct the Copernicus CAMS global model with machine learning, explain their drivers, and estimate where the pollution comes from.

Research prototype — not medical advice. Forecasts can be wrong. Always follow official air-quality advisories from your local authorities.
